# Clout Ranch — One-Pager GDD
**Platform:** Roblox · **Genre:** Idle collect + steal tycoon + social aura flex  
**Goal:** Viral discovery on X/TikTok + durable Robux revenue  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Pitch
You run a tiny ranch of **original Clout Pets**. They print cash while parked. Rival players can **steal** them when your ranch is unlocked. Every few minutes the lobby runs an **Aura Flex** — a 20-second pose/drip contest that awards Aura and spotlight clips. Weekly live-ops drop new pet skins tied to whatever is loud on X (sports memes, sounds, toy crazes) — always original art, never licensed IP.

## Why this, not a clone
| Proven loop | Our upgrade |
|-------------|-------------|
| Steal a Brainrot collect/steal/rebirth | Fairer new-player lock + clip-ready steal cam |
| Grow a Garden rarity chase | Pets + aura social layer, not just idle plant |
| Dress to Impress / aura trend | Short Aura Flex rounds baked into every session |
| Trend-chasing games | Weekly pet seasons instead of one-meme games |

**Won’t do:** Named Italian-brainrot characters (lawsuit risk), NFL/49ers marks, romance-scam sims, or “watch AI feed for speed” (Roblox banned that pattern).

## Core loop (60–90s dopamine)
1. Buy a Clout Pet from the **Carpet** (rotating stock).
2. Slot it in your ranch → it generates cash.
3. Spend cash on locks, traps, slap gear, and rarer pets.
4. Raid unlocked ranches → steal → sprint home.
5. **Rebirth** for permanent multipliers + more slots.
6. Hit **Aura Flex** when the server timer fires → flex drip/pets for votes → win limited Aura cosmetics.

## Monetization (honest, free-to-win)
| Product | Role |
|---------|------|
| 2x Cash | Early spend, stacks modestly |
| VIP | Lock time + income + tag |
| Limited Aura drip | Cosmetics only — no power |
| Weekly Secret Pet packs | FOMO live-ops, not required to compete |
| Lucky Block (dev product) | Random Common–Rare only at MVP |

Power that matters (rebirth, skill, timing) stays earnable. Cosmetics and convenience are the Robux sink.
