# Clout Ranch — Unique Selling Points

What no other Brainrot / steal-idle clone has — and why players (and creators) stay.

## 1. Aura Flex (social spectacle every ~3 min)

Rivals park pets and print cash. We add a **scheduled drip contest**: 20s flex → emoji/vote → 8s podium with gold/purple stage beams.

- Built into the session rhythm (not a side menu).
- Clip-native: podium poses + killfeed-style names = vertical video bait.
- Aura currency + cosmetics sink without selling win-condition power.

**Clone gap:** most steals are AFK loops. Flex makes every lobby a show.

## 2. Weekly trend pets (live culture pipeline)

Roster is **original Clout Pets** (24 at MVP). Live-ops drop **new original skins** tied to whatever is loud in kid internet culture that week — sports memes, sounds, toy crazes — never licensed IP. **Never name X/Twitter/TikTok in player-facing copy** (most Roblox kids don’t have those accounts).

- SeasonService / trend banner hooks keep the game culturally current.
- Creators get a weekly drop calendar + clip template.
- Avoids “one-meme game dies in 30 days.”

**Clone gap:** static meme rosters go stale; we refresh without lawsuit bait.

## 3. Fair VIP (time & comfort, not pay-to-win)

VIP buys **lock duration** (45s→120s) and a chrome/gold badge — **not** idle income mult, unstealable ranches, or exclusive Secret pity.

- Steal vs VIP still works a meaningful % of the time (target ≥30% successful contested steals).
- Power that matters (rebirth mults, timing, slap skill) stays earnable.
- Honest SKUs: 2x Cash, VIP QoL, Aura cosmetics, weekly Secret packs as FOMO — not required to compete.

**Clone gap:** many clones sell “you can’t play.” We sell drip and time.

## Positioning one-liner

> Steal-idle pet tycoon where **clout is the resource**, **Aura Flex is the spectacle**, and **every week internet culture becomes a new pet** — without ripping IP.
