# Clout Ranch — Art Direction (from Art agent)
Mobile-first. No licensed IP.

## Palette (six)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Gold drip | `#F5C84B` | Money, flex, top rarity |
| Deep purple | `#5B2A9E` | Night ranch, VIP, secondary brand |
| Soft lilac | `#C9A6FF` | UI fill, rare glow underlayer |
| Warm cream | `#FFF6E0` | Pet/type readability |
| Night void | `#1A1228` | Ground/sky contrast (not pure black) |
| Heat pink | `#FF4DB8` | Flex pop, Legendary+ flash only |

**Rule:** gold + purple own the brand. Cream = readability. Heat pink is rare, never wallpaper.

## Ranch identity
Sunset-to-night clout farm: gold fences, purple haze, drip puddles. Rounded Roblox volumes + metallic gold trim + purple neon edges — luxury barn, not horror. Landmarks read at phone distance in <1s: big silhouette, one accent, no texture noise.

## Pet silhouettes
**DO:** one readable animal outline at 64px; chunky limbs; cream belly or gold trim on purple; rarity = thin rim only; Mythic+ idle bob + slow drip trail.
**DON'T:** busy face decals; thin sticks; brand/IP shapes; glitter-blob accessory stacks; same silhouette across rarities.

## Rarity rims (mobile)
- Common: cream + muted purple, no glow
- Uncommon: soft lilac rim
- Rare: gold rim
- Epic: gold rim + soft purple underglow
- Legendary: gold + heat-pink pulse (slow)
- Mythic: rim + one drip particle from feet (capped)

## Aura Flex stage
Gold disc + purple neon ring. Night void + tall gold pillars. Flex: slight camera push-in, pet lifts 0.5s, gold→lilac shock ring, cream type + gold outline stamp. Clear other VFX during flex window.

## Signature VFX
1. Hatch drip (gold pour → pet)
2. First flex shock-ring + purple afterimage
3. Trade/steal slap: cream flash + short gold coin arc
4. Ranch level-up: fence purple→gold chase
5. Mythic entrance: heat-pink sky flash + local gold drip rain 1.5s

## Kill list
Licensed lookalikes, particle soup on Common, dark-on-dark pets, rectangle gray UI, cluttered nameplates.
